# Alec Radford

Researcher at [[openai]]. Lead author of [[language-understanding-gpt|Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training]] (GPT, 2018), which established the [[pre-train-then-fine-tune]] paradigm for [[transformer]]-based language understanding. Also lead author of GPT-2 (2019), which demonstrated emergent zero-shot capabilities with scale.
