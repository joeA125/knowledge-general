# Diederik P. Kingma

Researcher at [[openai]] (at time of publication). Co-creator of the Variational Autoencoder (VAE; Kingma & Welling, 2013), the Adam optimiser, and Inverse Autoregressive Flow. Co-author of the [[variational-lossy-autoencoders|Variational Lossy Autoencoder]] (2017).
