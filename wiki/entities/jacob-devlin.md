# Jacob Devlin

Researcher at Google AI Language. Lead author of [[bert-bidirectional-transformers|BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding]] (2019), which introduced the [[masked-language-model]] objective and established [[bert]] as the dominant paradigm for NLU tasks (2019–2021). BERT achieved SOTA on 11 NLP benchmarks including GLUE (80.5), SQuAD v1.1 (93.2 F1), and SWAG (86.3%).
