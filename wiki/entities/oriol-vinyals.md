# Oriol Vinyals

Researcher at [[google-brain]]. Lead author of [[pointer-networks|Pointer Networks]] (2015), which introduced the [[pointer-network]] architecture. Lead author of [[sequence-to-sequence-sets|Order Matters: Sequence to Sequence for Sets]] (2016), which introduced the [[read-process-write]] architecture. Co-author of [[rnn-regularisation|Recurrent Neural Network Regularization]] (2014). Also co-authored "Grammar as a Foreign Language" and "Show and Tell" (neural image captioning).
