# Vladlen Koltun

Researcher at Intel Labs. Co-author of [[context-aggregation-dilated-convolutions|Multi-Scale Context Aggregation by Dilated Convolutions]] (2016). Also authored work on efficient inference in fully connected CRFs (Krähenbühl & Koltun, 2011), used in many semantic segmentation systems.
