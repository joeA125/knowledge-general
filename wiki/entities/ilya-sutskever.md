# Ilya Sutskever

Researcher at [[google-brain]] and later co-founder and Chief Scientist of [[openai]]. Co-author of [[rnn-regularisation|Recurrent Neural Network Regularization]] (2014), co-developer of sequence-to-sequence learning (Sutskever et al., 2014), co-author of the [[variational-lossy-autoencoders|Variational Lossy Autoencoder]] (2017), and co-author of [[language-understanding-gpt|GPT]] (2018), which established the [[pre-train-then-fine-tune]] paradigm for Transformer-based language understanding. A key figure in the development of modern deep learning.
