# Kaiming He

Researcher at [[microsoft-research]]. Lead author of the ResNet papers: "Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition" (2016) and [[identity-mapping-residual-networks|Identity Mappings in Deep Residual Networks]] (2016). Introduced [[residual-connections]] and the [[pre-activation-resnet]]. Also developed the Kaiming weight initialisation method.
