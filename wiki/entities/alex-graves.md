# Alex Graves

Researcher at [[google-deepmind]]. Lead author of [[neural-turing-machines|Neural Turing Machines]] (2014), which introduced the [[neural-turing-machine]] architecture. Also known for work on sequence generation with RNNs, handwriting generation, CTC for speech recognition, and Differentiable Neural Computers.
